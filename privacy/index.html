Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026 · Applies to the PixStudio app for Android and iOS.

In short: PixStudio does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app works fully offline, and your photos never leave your device.

1. Information we collect

None. PixStudio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location, contacts or usage statistics.

2. Your photos and projects

Photos you open are processed only on your device. Edits, background removal and every other feature run locally on your phone's processor and graphics chip. Projects are auto-saved in the app's private storage on your device so you can continue editing later; they are deleted when you delete the project or uninstall the app. Exported images are saved only where you choose: your gallery, or the app you share to.

3. Network access

PixStudio does not send your photos or any other data over the internet. On Android, the on-device background-removal model is provided by Google Play services, which may download that model to your device; this download is handled by Google Play services under Google's privacy policy and does not include your photos.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
CameraOnly when you tap Camera, to take a photo to edit.
Photos / mediaOnly to open a photo you pick. On recent Android and iOS versions the system photo picker shares just the photo you select.
Storage / Add to photo libraryOnly to save your finished image to your gallery in a “PixStudio” album.

You can deny or revoke any permission in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

5. Sharing

When you tap a share button (for example WhatsApp or Instagram), the exported image is handed to that app through your device's standard share system. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

6. Children

PixStudio is a general-audience app. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
